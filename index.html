2 · Company logo library

Select a production company found through TMDB to automatically retrieve its available logo, or upload a transparent logo. Logos persist in this browser's local storage.

3 · Awards

Award references are searched automatically when you select a title. Review source evidence and confirm outcomes before adding awards to artwork.

Import award records (CSV)

Import reviewed records with required columns award,outcome,year,category,recipient,source. Each record must include a valid ceremony year, category, recipient and evidence URL; PAS does not independently verify the source. Review each record before adding it. Supported awards: Oscar, Emmy, SAG, Golden Globe, Razzie.

4 · Remove original text

Paint over lettering to remove it using a local pixel-repair method. This is not generative AI inpainting; complex areas may require an external editor.

Repair uses surrounding pixel colors and can blur large regions. For precision, use a dedicated inpainting application.
